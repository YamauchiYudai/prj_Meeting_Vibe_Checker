# Product Overview

Meeting Vibe Checker is a tool designed to analyze and quantify the emotional tone and engagement levels of digital meetings.

## Core Capabilities

- **Real-time Sentiment Analysis**: Detect and visualize the "vibe" of a meeting as it happens.
- **Engagement Tracking**: Monitor participant activity and participation levels.
- **Vibe Reports**: Generate post-meeting summaries of the emotional trajectory and key moments.

## Target Use Cases

- **Remote Team Meetings**: Helping managers gauge team morale during syncs.
- **Workshops and Brainstorming**: Ensuring high energy and balanced participation.
- **Feedback Sessions**: Monitoring the tone of sensitive discussions.

## Value Proposition

Helps facilitators and participants understand unspoken dynamics, ensuring more inclusive and effective meeting outcomes through data-driven insights.
